# Final Demo Script

This script presents UsahaNaik as a local-first Android portfolio product for Indonesian UMKM owners.

## Opening

UsahaNaik helps UMKM owners organize business data, track simple finances, follow weekly actions, plan content, review progress, and generate local business reports. It is local-first, rule-based, and AI-ready, but this version does not include real backend authentication, cloud sync, or paid AI integration.

## Demo Flow

1. Start at Welcome.
2. Show Indonesian and English language selection.
3. Open Login/Register briefly and explain they are auth-ready placeholders.
4. Continue in local mode.
5. Open Profile and load demo data for `Dapur Rasa Nusantara`.
6. Return to Dashboard and show:
   - business health summary
   - financial KPI cards
   - dashboard financial source label
   - weekly plan progress
   - content workflow summary
   - report and reminder cards
7. Add an income or expense entry and point out local metric refresh.
8. Open Weekly Plan, mark one task complete, and show progress changes.
9. Open Ideas, generate deterministic local ideas, save one, and schedule it.
10. Open Report and show KPI, chart summaries, and export-ready report text.
11. Open Profile, show language switch, reminders, demo controls, and local-first notes.

## Closing Positioning

UsahaNaik is a portfolio-ready local Android product foundation. It demonstrates Compose UI, Room persistence, ViewModels, deterministic business logic, notification-ready reminders, bilingual app copy, and product-safe AI architecture.

## Do Not Claim

- Real authentication.
- Cloud sync.
- Paid AI or real remote AI generation.
- Guaranteed sales or profit increase.
- Professional financial, accounting, tax, legal, or investment advice.
